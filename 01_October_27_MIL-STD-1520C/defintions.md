# MIL-STD-1520C: Corrective Action and Disposition System for Nonconforming Material

## Overview

**MIL-STD-1520C** is a United States Department of Defense military standard titled *"Corrective Action and Disposition System for Nonconforming Material"*. It establishes the requirements for a systematic approach to identifying, documenting, correcting, and preventing the recurrence of nonconformances in products, processes, and services supplied to the Department of Defense.

First published in the 1970s, MIL-STD-1520C represents one of the foundational quality management standards that influenced the development of the Eight Disciplines (8D) problem-solving methodology. It provides the framework for corrective action systems that are essential in defense contracting, aerospace, and other highly regulated industries.

## Relationship to Eight Disciplines (8D)

MIL-STD-1520C is considered a precursor to and influence on the Eight Disciplines methodology. The structured approach required by the military standard laid the groundwork for the more formalized 8D process developed by Ford Motor Company in the 1980s.

### Parallels Between MIL-STD-1520C and 8D

| MIL-STD-1520C Requirement | 8D Discipline |
|---------------------------|---------------|
| Problem identification and documentation | D2: Describe the Problem |
| Containment and immediate action | D3: Interim Containment Actions |
| Root cause investigation | D4: Root Cause Analysis |
| Corrective action planning | D5: Choose Permanent Corrective Actions |
| Implementation | D6: Implement Corrective Actions |
| Effectiveness verification | D7: Prevent Recurrence |
| Trending and management review | D8: Recognize the Team / Systemic Improvement |

## Evolution and Current Status

### Historical Development

- **MIL-STD-1520** (1974): Original standard establishing corrective action requirements
- **MIL-STD-1520A** (1979): First revision with enhanced documentation requirements
- **MIL-STD-1520B** (1986): Added emphasis on root cause analysis and effectiveness verification
- **MIL-STD-1520C** (1995): Current version with streamlined requirements and modern quality practices
